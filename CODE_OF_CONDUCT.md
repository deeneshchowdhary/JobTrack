# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in JobTrack a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's GitHub security
advisory form. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions or
participation that violate these standards. This policy applies in all project
spaces and when someone officially represents the project publicly.
